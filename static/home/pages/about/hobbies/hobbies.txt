{c#ff00ff,18}Violin{c#ff00ff,18}
I am an amateur violinist and have been playing since childhood.  At one point, I seriously considered going to conservatory and pursuing it professionally.
{iviolin1.jpg,300,My violin}  {icima.jpg,300,One of the first violin sonatas ever published, by Giovanni Cima}  {iviolin2.jpg,300,The slightly cursed tuning required for Biber Sonata n. 11}

These day's I mostly specialize in Baroque violin, a type of period playing from around 1600-1750.  While similar to modern violin, the Baroque style utilizes a violin of slightly different construction and technique.

If you're looking for reccomendations from that period, {bHeinrich Biber's Rosary Sonatas,https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jd2wYGeGsn4&list=PLvszsPl-ZIE6zfwDQ5RafOT3ObUOQ6CA_,#ffffff} are some of my favorite pieces of all time, especially number 16.
