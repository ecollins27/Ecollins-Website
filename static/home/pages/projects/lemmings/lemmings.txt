{c#ffff00}DESCRIPTION{c#ffff00}
        A recreation of the 1991 game "Lemmings" with some creative liberties.

{c#ffff00}INSPIRATION{c#ffff00}
        One of my first ever coding projects was an attempted recreating of Lemmings!  Of course, having maybe 3 months of experience in Java, the project was a mess: slow, confusing, and undocumented.  However, I loved the idea and wanted to come back to it, birthing this project 3 years later!
