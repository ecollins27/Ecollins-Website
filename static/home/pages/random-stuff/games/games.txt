{c#ff00ff,24}GAMES{c#ff00ff,24}

I will occasionally make small ASCII games in Javascript.  Nothing major, just a little 1 day project to test my Javascript skills.

I maintain a leaderboard of high scores for each game.  See if you can beat the top score!


{bSnake,snake,#ff0000} - Use W,A,S,D to control the snake and eat apples.  Don't run into yourself or the walls!

{bTetris,tetris,#00ffff} - It's Tetris

{bMaze,maze,#00ff00} - Navigate the randomly generated maze from the top left to bottom right in as little time as possible.
